# fir must be able to reach any kind of hardware

Status: accepted
Tier: direction; area: semantics

## Decision

- **G0.** fir must be able to reach any kind of hardware: CPUs first, but
  also GPUs, NPUs, TPUs and hardware we cannot predict yet, such as photonic
  or quantum processors. No milestone targets non-CPU hardware yet.
- **A small core, open by construction.** The core stays a small CPU-shaped
  dialect. Other targets come in as extension dialects. The openness lives in
  how fir is built, not in what the core contains.
- **A standing check.** Every decision, now and later, is checked against
  G0, and none may close that door.
- **What it asks of the structures.** Types and ops come from dialects, so
  there is no closed core enum. `funk-ir` holds ops generically and checks
  them through a registry in which each dialect registers its ops. The Lean
  semantics is parameterized over the dialect, with ops, types and effects
  coming in through typeclasses, and its proofs are stated over the dialect
  where they can be. One text grammar covers every dialect. Refinement is
  defined over observations that each dialect supplies. Symbol tables can
  nest, such as a device module inside a host module. Memory has several
  memories, dialects add types and attributes as well as ops, and the
  verifier has a hook for use-once values, which quantum dialects need.

[semantics.md](../decisions/semantics.md) states what holds and the
decisions that apply G0: ADR-2026-10-30-1146, ADR-2026-11-03-0600,
ADR-2026-11-09-1322, ADR-2026-11-09-1542, ADR-2026-11-09-1543,
ADR-2026-11-09-1544 and ADR-2026-11-09-2257. The human set G0 on 2026-10-29
(#5).

## Why

Funk is a reusable toolchain, in the spirit of LLVM, and its users will
target more than CPUs. Structures fixed early, such as a closed list of ops
or a grammar with one production per op, are costly to open once code
depends on them. M0 pays for a registry, a parameterized semantics and a
generic grammar, and checks that a closed enum would give for free, such as
an op's operand count, move into the registry and the verifier.

## Rejected alternatives

- **A closed core:** a Rust `enum` of core ops, one closed Lean `inductive`
  and a grammar with one production per op. Each later target would reopen
  the IR, the semantics and the parser once code depends on them.
